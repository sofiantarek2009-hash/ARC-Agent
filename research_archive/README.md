# ARC-AGI-3 Comprehensive Research Archive

Welcome to the definitive research archive for the **ARC Prize 2026 (ARC-AGI-3)** fluid-intelligence agent. This archive preserves the full, unabridged findings, mathematical derivations, competitive intelligence, and architectural blueprints produced by 23 specialized research agents and theorists across all phases of development.
